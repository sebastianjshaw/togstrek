Some friends and I headed out after work on Friday and found a nice little campsite on the waters of Stora Kroksjön. We set up camp which became our base for the weekend for easy hikes around the area, a little swimming for the tougher amongst us and generally just relaxing in nature.

If [Gothenburg](/europe/sweden/vastra-gotland/gothenburg) does one thing well, it is sunsets.
