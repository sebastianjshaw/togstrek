Climbing [Kilimanjaro](/africa/tanzania/mt-kilimanjaro) via the Machame Route is one of the most popular ways to the summit of Africa’s highest mountain and the world’s highest free-standing mountain. The Machame Route, also known as the “Whiskey Route,” is a tough trek that takes you through varied landscapes and ecosystems, providing stunning views along the way.
