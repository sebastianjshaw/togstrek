## Trail Information

Simple trails, well graveled in most parts with various shops and cafes on the route as it reaches Skatås. There are a few steep climbs, and some nice water crossings on well made bridges.

## Accommodation

This was done as a day hike, so no personal information on where to camp.

## Sights

The main thing to see is Skatås and Delsjon lake as you walk by.
