## Trail Information

**Distance**: 9.7 km

**Difficulty**: Moderate

**Transport**: There is a bus stop right by Kåsjön called Åstebo and Skatås is a very well connected point being close to [Gothenburg](/europe/sweden/vastra-gotland/gothenburg). The nearest public transport stop being Welandersgatan

**Terrain:** A mix of dirt roads and forest paths with an increase in elevation of 236 m

* * *

## Accommodation & Shopping

### Vindskydd Getryggen

With incredible views, Getryggen’s windshelter, with fire pit and benches is a popular stopping point. Much of the terrain is solid rock so pegging a tent maybe a challenge.

### Vindskydd Prästtjärn

Somewhat off the main trail, Prästtjärn’s windshelter is on the banks of a lake.

## Sights

### Getryggen

Perhaps three kilometers past Skatås , you reach the view point and Getryggen, giving you views back over Gothenburg.

## Side Trails & Add-ons

This close to the city, there are a lot of trails crossing Bohusleden and as such it can be a challenge to keep on the right trail at times!

* * *

## The Experience

Taking the 512 bus from Liseberg station directly to the start/end of the trail, this is a good 10km hike through forests which won’t stress too many hikers out. There are a decent number of points to stop at with tables or benches and the trail is mostly through wooded terrain though the parts near Skatås are more gravel trails.
