## Trail Information

This section of Bohusleden is only 8 kilometres long and is of moderate difficulty. Lots of dirt trails and gravel tracks make the eight kilometres fly by. Several lovely lakes dot the hike's distance.

**Distance**: 8 Kilometres

**Difficulty**: Moderate

**Transport**:

**Terrain:** Lakes and gravel

* * *

## Accommodation & Shopping

This was done as a day hike, so no personal information on where to camp.

## Sights

Kåsjön is one of many lakes you pass during this hike; the calm water reflects the deep blue Swedish sky in good weather.

Depending on your preference, Jonsered's Factories and courtyards can be explored at the end or beginning of the trail, seeing one of the reasons for the town of Jonsered existence. Build on the rivers to allow boat access for moving goods and the mill power they provide.
