With Corona making travel impossible and irresponsible, I had the chance to take a month off work in the summer and hike the entire 443 km of the King’s Trail. Starting in the far north of Sweden, the Arctic town of Abisko, and ending nearer the middle in Hemavan, the trail moves through diverse terrain and is an incredible chance to really see what Sweden has to offer.

The stage write-ups that follow are from that through-hike — where the path went, what the weather did, and what the huts and crossings were like in practice.
