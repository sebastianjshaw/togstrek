## Trail Information

**Distance**: 4.7km

**Difficulty**: Easy

**Transport**: Train, Bus & Airport are all options at Örnsköldsvik

* * *

## Accommodation & Shopping

Örnsköldsvik has plenty of accommodation options at various price points.

## Side Trails

You’re in the final strech (or the starting hike) and so there’s little to do but get to the end.

## The Experience

### Day 1

Diversion signs and a tired decision to stay on the main road swallowed most of this leg; I doubt the official path would have felt dramatically different from the shoulder I actually walked.
