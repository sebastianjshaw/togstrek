## Sights & Culture

### Tulcan Cemetary

A strange place.

The hundreds of trees lining the cemetery are all sculpted into Incan style statues. Initially started by a single person, creating topiary of the Incan Artefacts he had seen it has grown to be the dominant feature of the cemetery, bringing in tourists.

Strangely, you rent a spot in the stacked remains for a period before you are 'moved' to a less prominent space.
