## Sights & Culture

### Santuario de la Santísima Virgen del Rocío

The views from the top over the town are great, though the weather was poor, so visibility was limited.

The very top houses a dome, visible from almost anywhere in Biblián.

The Church was a significant amount of effort to build but is now a remarkable sight for visitors and locals alike.
