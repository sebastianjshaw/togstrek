With its stunning views of the Tarentaise valley, it attracts skiers and snowboarders from all over the world. The ski area offers a wide range of slopes, from easy blues to challenging blacks, making it suitable for skiers and snowboarders of all levels. It also features a variety of après-ski activities, such as shopping, dining, and nightlife, ensuring that visitors have a memorable experience both on and off the slopes. In addition, Peisey-Vallandry ski area is connected to the vast Paradiski ski area, providing skiers with even more slopes to explore.
