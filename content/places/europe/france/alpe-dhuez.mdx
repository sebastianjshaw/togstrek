Based in the Alpe d’Huez area, our home was Les Arcs 2000 which is part of the Les Arcs ski area, which encompasses four interconnected resorts with a total of 425 kilometres of incredible ski slopes. Les Arcs 2000 is known for its fantastic social life, high-speed lift systems, and excellent ski conditions. The resort offers a wide range of slopes for skiers and snowboarders from gentle slopes for beginners to challenging runs for experts including the longest Black in the world, La Sarenne. The resort has an infamous après-ski scene, with a wide selection of bars, restaurants, and clubs to choose from.
