Helsingør, also known as Elsinore, is a port city in eastern Denmark. Overlooking the Øresund strait, the 15th-century **Kronborg Castle** provided the setting for Shakespeare’s *Hamlet*. Nearby, the M/S Maritime Museum of Denmark illustrates 600 years of Danish seafaring history. The glass-walled **Culture Yard** in the old shipyard hosts cultural events. On the pier is **“Han,”** a polished steel sculpture of a man.

## Sights & Culture

### Helsingør Rådhus

Helsingør City Hall is a large red stone building near the waterfront. Worth a visit for the architecture, but not somewhere to spend too much time.

<div className="togstrek-place-mdx-photo-feature-wrap">

![Helsingør city hall](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/denmark/helsingor/Helsingor-0002.jpg)

</div>

### Havnekranen

The harbour crane on the waterfront is a visual reminder of Helsingør’s past, much like the Göteborg cranes — though on a far smaller scale.

<div className="togstrek-place-mdx-photo-feature-wrap">

![Harbour crane, Helsingør](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/denmark/helsingor/Helsingor-0001.jpg)

</div>

### Sankt Olai Kirke

An old Lutheran church in old town Helsingør — red brick and a green bronze roof easily seen from across the water, and a clear landmark within the city.

### Sankt Mariæ Kirke

Not far from Sankt Olai is St Maria’s Church, a 15th-century monastery famed for its artwork and organ; it now serves as a Lutheran church.

### Han statue

In the harbour, on the dock, sits the Han statue, sibling to Copenhagen’s Little Mermaid. If you are patient and watch long enough, you may see him blink.

### Holger Danske

Likely the most famous statue in Helsingør, Holger the Dane sits enthroned below the castle. Changing spotlights pick out different parts of this huge stone figure. The route through the castle basement is easy to miss — it is worth the extra effort to find him and see more of the fortifications underground.

### Kronborg Castle

<div className="togstrek-place-mdx-photo-feature-wrap">

![Kronborg Castle from the harbour](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/denmark/helsingor/Castle-0003.jpg)

Kronborg is a 16th-century castle and the model for Elsinore in *Hamlet*. Visible from much of the city and from **Helsingborg** in Sweden across the strait, it sits on a peninsula in the harbour.

In summer, scenes from *Hamlet* are performed in the grounds, and the banquet halls and rooms are open to visitors with a ticket so you can see the historic furnishing.

Outside the walls, cafés, restaurants, and shops fill the old buildings between the outer moat and the castle. This is a **traditional castle** rather than the manor-house style you often get under the name *slott* — the jewel of Helsingør and the main reason to visit.

Climbing the tower gives a strong view back over the city, though the narrow stone steps can be a challenge. The gift shop is large and stocks a very wide range of souvenirs.

### Kronborg Slotskirke

Set in one of the castle walls, the chapel feels ancient — dark wood and a heavy atmosphere that is hard to describe.

### Herakles og Hydraen

On the dock, facing the water, Hercules fights the Hydra.

## Museums & Galleries

### Danmarks Tekniske Museum

Further out of town — roughly twenty minutes by bus — the Technology Museum is filled with flight exhibits and could pass for an aerospace museum. On one visit there was also a hall on the history of video games, with many playable machines.

<div className="togstrek-place-mdx-photo-feature-wrap">

![Danmarks Tekniske Museum](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/denmark/helsingor/Technology+Museum-0001.jpg)

</div>

### Øresundsakvariet

The Øresund Aquarium is tiny: a pair of rooms lined with exotic and local fish and crustaceans. The staff are passionate, and the venue acts as a base for wider marine activities — diving, wild camping, and cooking.

<div className="togstrek-place-mdx-photo-feature-wrap">

![Øresund Aquarium collage](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/denmark/helsingor/BeFunky-collage.jpg)

</div>

Well worth a visit — but don’t go expecting SeaWorld.

### M/S Museet for Søfart

Below ground, on the way to the castle, the maritime museum charts the seafaring history of the city and country. Highlights include sitting in a tattoo chair while a sailor’s tattoo is projected onto your arm for tracing in pen — it’s easily worth a visit.

## Parks & Gardens

### Danserindebrønden

The Dancer’s Fountain is a sculpture of three ballet dancers — a favourite stop to see butterflies in summer.

## Restaurants & Bars

### Københavneren

In the centre of the old town, with a charming beer garden — local dishes and a very wide range of beers.

### Strandvejsristeriet

A small café near the castle, inside the outer walls — excellent coffee and home-baked goods.

### Elsinore Street Food

Similar in spirit to Copenhagen’s street-food halls but smaller: craft beer, coffee, and varied food in a warehouse space with DIY furniture vibes.
