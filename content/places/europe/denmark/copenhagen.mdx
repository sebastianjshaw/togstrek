### Freetown Christiania

<div className="togstrek-place-mdx-photo-feature-wrap">

![Freetown Christiania](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/denmark/copenhagen/20160903+-+Copenhagen+-+009.jpg)

</div>

Freetown **Christiania** grew out of an old military base taken over decades ago by a commune that built housing, workspaces, and venues for art and music — idealistic and partly outside ordinary law.

More recently, organised crime has been tied to the cannabis trade in the area, with serious incidents; signs outside have urged visitors to bring their own rather than buy on site. Even so, on a calm day it can feel like an open-air art quarter: odd murals, casual cafés, and home-brewed drinks.

### Christiansborg Palace

The seat of government for Copenhagen is **Christiansborg Palace** — an impressive, deliberately solid block of a building on Slotsholmen.

### Kastellet

From the air, **Kastellet** is unmistakable: a star-shaped citadel on its own island in a moat. On a map it almost looks like a level from a real-time strategy game rather than real urban fabric.

### Den Lille Havfrue at Langelinie

The **Little Mermaid** at Langelinie is world-famous — and almost everyone comments on how small the bronze figure is in person.

## Museums & Galleries

### SMK – Statens Museum for Kunst

The **National Gallery of Denmark** is vast; the curatorial reach even spills outdoors — including seating that sits in the water by the lakes.

## Restaurants & Bars

### Street Food Market at Christianshavn

For a varied meal in one place, **Christianshavn’s street food hall** (mostly indoors) lines up stalls across cuisines and drinks — the kind of spot where you wish you had another day to work through the menu.
