Akureyri, often referred to as the "Capital of North Iceland," is a vibrant town that combines urban sophistication with stunning natural beauty. Nestled at the base of Eyjafjörður Fjord, Akureyri is known for its picturesque setting, charming streets, and a wealth of cultural and recreational activities. Whether you're exploring its modern architecture, visiting the lush botanical gardens, or embarking on a thrilling whale watching tour, Akureyri offers a rich and diverse experience.

## Sights & Culture

### Kattarhryggur

Kattarhryggur, known as "Cat's Spine" in English, is a remarkable geological formation in Iceland. Located in the rugged and scenic region of the Westfjords, this unique ridge resembles the spine of a cat, with its jagged and undulating appearance. The name perfectly captures the ridge's distinct shape, which stands out against the dramatic landscape.

![](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/iceland/akureyri/20230730-Kattarhryggur-20230730-003A0669-HDR.jpg)

It was here we stopped, and offered candy and good vibes to _Huldufólk,_ the Hidden People, the elves of Iceland. We hope they enjoyed our offering, and if the weather was an indication then they definitely did.

### Church

Akureyri Church, with its strikingly modern design, stands out against the traditional Icelandic architecture that surrounds it. Currently undergoing renovations, with the steps being replaced, this prominent landmark is known for its unique combination of contemporary style and traditional elements. Inside, the church offers a serene space filled with beautiful stained glass windows and intricate woodwork, making it a peaceful retreat in the heart of the town.

## Parks & Gardens

### Botanical Garden

The Akureyri Botanical Garden is a vibrant and colourful oasis, offering a stunning array of plants and flowers even in the colder months. In winter, the garden transforms into a picturesque snow-covered wonderland. The interesting band pavilion within the garden adds a charming touch, making it a perfect spot for a leisurely stroll or a quiet moment of reflection amidst nature's beauty.

## Restaurants & Bars

### Centrum

Centrum is a modern and stylish café located in the heart of Akureyri. Known for its excellent coffee and beer selection, as well as a range of premium wines, it’s a popular spot for both locals and visitors. The chic atmosphere and friendly service make it an ideal place to unwind after a day of exploring.

### Botanical Gardens Cafe

The Botanical Gardens Café is very modern and hip, providing a serene setting within the lush surroundings of the gardens. Offering really good coffee and a selection of beers and premium wines, the café is a great place to relax and enjoy the natural beauty around you. Its stylish decor and tranquil ambiance make it a delightful spot for a break.

### Dinner Buffet

For a high-end dining experience, the dinner buffet in Akureyri is highly recommended. Though expensive, the buffet offers a wide variety of gourmet dishes, showcasing the best of Icelandic cuisine. The quality and presentation of the food make it worth the splurge, providing a memorable culinary experience.

## Shoppping

Akureyri offers a range of shopping options, from unique souvenirs to high-quality cold weather gear. The town's shops are perfect for finding gifts or gear to keep you warm during your Icelandic adventures. The blend of local craftsmanship and modern amenities ensures that you’ll find something special to take home.

## Sports & Activities

### Whale Watching

Whale watching in Akureyri is an unforgettable experience. During one tour, we were fortunate to spot three humpback whales, accompanied by the entertaining commentary of a funny guide. The tour also offered sightings of various birds, adding to the excitement. However, be prepared for cold and heavy winds, so dress warmly to fully enjoy this incredible adventure amidst Iceland's natural marine beauty.
