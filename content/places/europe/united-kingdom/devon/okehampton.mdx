## Sights & Culture

### Okehampton Castle

About half an hours drive from our cottage was Okehampton Castle, which was unfortunately closed for the winter months. A ruin, but one on an impressive location at the summit of a hill, it gives a sense of what it would take to attach such a castle when you see the steep edges of the hill. Unable to visit the castle grounds, we contented ourselves with a nature walk in the local area.
