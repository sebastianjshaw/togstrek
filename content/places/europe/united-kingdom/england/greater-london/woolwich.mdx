Woolwich, located in the Royal Borough of Greenwich, is an area rich in history and undergoing significant regeneration. Historically known for its military and industrial heritage, Woolwich was home to the Royal Arsenal, a key site for munitions production and innovation from the 17th century onwards. Today, this heritage is celebrated through the Royal Arsenal Riverside development, which has transformed former military buildings into modern residential, commercial, and cultural spaces.

The area boasts several points of interest, including the Woolwich Ferry, which provides free transportation across the River Thames, and the Woolwich Dockyard, which dates back to the Tudor period. The bustling Woolwich Market offers a variety of fresh produce, street food, and local goods, reflecting the area’s diverse community.

Woolwich is also home to the newly opened Woolwich Works, a creative district that hosts performances, exhibitions, and community events in beautifully restored historic buildings. The nearby Thames Path provides scenic walking routes along the river, offering views of London's skyline and access to parks and green spaces.
