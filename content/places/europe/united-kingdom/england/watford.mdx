Watford, nestled in the heart of Hertfordshire, England, is a town that effortlessly blends the hustle and bustle of urban life with the charm of the English countryside. Just a brief journey from [London](/europe/united-kingdom/england/greater-london/london), Watford offers a rich history that stretches back to Roman times, making it a treasure trove of fascinating tales and architectural wonders. From the tranquillity of Cassiobury Park to the lively shopping scene at the Intu Watford Shopping Centre, Watford is a town full of surprises.

### The Making of Harry Potter

With a few friends, I took the day off and went to the Warner Brothers Studios, for the Harry Potter World tour. It is a remarkable place. The detail on so many of the props that are never seen other than the corner of your eye is remarkable. A display of ice cream in the window of a shop Harry uses in his first film.
