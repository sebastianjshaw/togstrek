### Sandsfoot Castle

Henry VIII built the castle between 1539 and 1541 as part of the coastal defences against invasion. It stands on a cliff above Portland Harbour.

### **Gardens and Surroundings**

The castle sits in Sandsfoot Gardens. The Rodwell Trail, a walking and cycling path along a former railway line, runs nearby.
