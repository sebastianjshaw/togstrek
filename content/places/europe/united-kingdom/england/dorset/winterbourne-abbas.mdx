Winterbourne Abbas is a small village located in Dorset, England, known for its charming rural setting and proximity to significant prehistoric sites. Nestled in the picturesque landscape of the South West, this village offers a peaceful retreat with a deep sense of history.

### **Nine Stones Stone Circle**

One of the most notable features of Winterbourne Abbas is the Nine Stones Stone Circle, an ancient monument dating back to the late Neolithic or early Bronze Age. This small stone circle, located just outside the village, consists of nine standing stones arranged in a rough oval. The site is one of the few surviving stone circles in Dorset and provides a fascinating glimpse into the prehistoric past. Visitors can easily access the site, which is set amidst serene fields and farmland.

### **Historic Charm**

The village itself is characterized by traditional English countryside charm. Its historic buildings, narrow lanes, and the tranquil environment make it an ideal place for leisurely walks and exploration. The local parish church, St. Mary’s, adds to the village's historical appeal, with its roots tracing back to medieval times.
