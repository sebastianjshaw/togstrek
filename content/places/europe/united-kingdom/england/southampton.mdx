A city that's a treasure trove of history, culture, and maritime marvels. Nestled on England's south coast, it's a place where the old and the new intertwine in a delightful dance. The city's rich history is etched into every corner, from the ancient city walls that whisper tales of the past, to the bustling port that has welcomed ships for centuries. Yet, it's not all about the past. Southampton is a vibrant, modern city, brimming with cutting-edge art, lively festivals, and a thriving music scene.

## Sports & Activities

### Paultons Park

#### Home of Peppa Pig World

Set in the New Forest, Paulton’s Park is somewhat overshadowed by the section dedicated to Peppa Pig. The Park dates from 1269 when John Paulton bought it from the local Abbot, and much more recently became a family amusement park. Offering a selection of rollercoasters rides for smaller children and a few animal enclosures Paulton’s can’t match Alton Towers or the Disney Worlds, but its a fun day out and generally a lot less busy than the bigger names.
