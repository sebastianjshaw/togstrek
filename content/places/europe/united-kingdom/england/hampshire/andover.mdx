With a rich history that dates back to the Saxon era, Andover is home to a number of fascinating historic sites and landmarks, including the beautiful St Mary's Church and the impressive Andover Guildhall. The town is also known for its vibrant arts and culture scene, with a number of galleries, museums and theaters showcasing local talent. Visitors can enjoy a leisurely stroll along the River Anton, indulge in some retail therapy at the bustling high street or sample some delicious local cuisine at one of the many restaurants and pubs.
