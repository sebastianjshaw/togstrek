Newcastle-under-Lyme, a historic market town in Staffordshire, England, is rich in both heritage and modern amenities. Nestled near the city of Stoke-on-Trent, it offers a charming blend of old and new. The town's centre boasts an array of Georgian architecture, quaint shops, and vibrant markets that hark back to its roots as a medieval trading hub. Culturally, Newcastle-under-Lyme is home to the New Vic Theatre, one of Europe’s most progressive producing theatres-in-the-round, providing a hub for contemporary and classical performances. The surrounding countryside and nearby parks, such as the scenic Queen's Gardens, offer a refreshing escape with their lush greenery and tranquil paths. With its unique mix of history, culture, and natural beauty, Newcastle-under-Lyme is a delightful destination for residents and visitors alike. It’s also the home to Keele University

### Keele University

The University of Keele, situated on a picturesque 600-acre campus in Staffordshire, is renowned for its innovative approach to higher education and research. Established in 1949, Keele prides itself on its interdisciplinary ethos, offering a unique dual honours system that allows students to study two subjects in depth. The campus, one of the largest in the UK, features beautiful parkland, woodlands, and lakes, providing a serene environment conducive to learning and reflection.
