## Sights & Culture

### Conwy Castle

Conwy Castle in Wales was built in the 13th century, and spent hundreds of years as a vital strategic and defensive location.  
The ramparts are walkable, and worth the climb.

Still in remarkable condition, it is a good place to explore and offers lovely views.
