Tucked away in the bay of Skälderviken, in the north-western corner of Scania, Sweden, you'll find the quaint locality of Arild. With a population of just over 500, Arild is a gem of a place that's steeped in history and natural beauty. The heart of Arild beats around its small harbour and the Arilds Chapel, both of which date back centuries. The village is also a haven for artists, with its picturesque landscapes serving as the perfect muse.

### Himmelstorpgården

A small homestead, with a cafe (closed at midsommar) of traditionally built buildings marks the start of the trail proper to Ladonia.

## Restaurants & Bars

### Arilds Vingård

I found this vineyard after taking a random turn following a sign for food on my way to Ladonia. It is a charming hotel, vineyard and restaurant offering locally produced and sourced food. I had the cheese and meat plate, which was excellent as was the service. Sat on an outdoor patio facing the vines, it was a lovely place for a meal.
