With its sprawling forests, serene lakes, and rolling hills, it's a landscape that seems to have been crafted straight from a fairy tale. Whether you're a seasoned hiker looking to conquer new trails, or a family seeking a tranquil picnic spot, Isaberg has something for everyone. In the winter, the region transforms into a snowy wonderland, with skiing and snowboarding opportunities aplenty.

## Sports & Activities

### Isaberg Mountain Resort

Isaberg mountain resort offers skiing and snowboarding in the winter and mountain sports in the summer. There is not much around it and getting there involves driving through a fair amount of forest, but it is a good location for a day downhill skiing within an hour and a half drive of [Gothenburg](/europe/sweden/vastra-gotaland/gothenburg). Unlike the nearer [Ulricehamn](https://app.fabrik.io/portfolio/vastra-gotaland), it can't be reached conveniently by public transport, but the slopes are better including one chair lift, a blessed relief from the T-Bars for any snowboarder in the party.
