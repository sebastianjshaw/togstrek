## Sports & Activities

### Romme Alpin Ski Resort

Much like [Branäs](/europe/sweden/brans), Romme Alpin is about five hours drive north of [Gothenburg](/europe/sweden/vastra-gotaland/gothenburg). Also like [Branäs](/europe/sweden/brans), there is little there other than skiing. The slopes are better than [Branäs](/europe/sweden/brans), though the apres-ski is more limited, and there are a series of efficient, but officious staff who collect up skis from outside the building at 5 pm regardless of if they are rented or owned, which can be an unpleasant surprise. The slopes have a lot of snow machines and a real effort is made, even in a mild winter to offer decent snow, but the difference between the two visits is marked.

![OnePlus ONEPLUS A6013, 4.3mm, f1.7, 1/100, ISO160](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/sweden/dalarna/romme-alpin/d495aa98d8f6b4e7.jpg)

Accommodation is more limited near the slopes, being focused on the resort hotel. We stayed in a nice cabin on both occasions, but it was about ten minutes drive from the slopes.
