Known for generations for its leather, Malung is still the place to go for leatherwork in the middle of Sweden. More stores are a little outside of the town, but it's easy to find them, just follow the main road north. A fairly small place with two super markets supermarket, off licence and a few shops, it's a reasonable hours drive from Sälen, making it a good stopping point for the evening to get an early start on the slopes.

## Museums & Galleries

### Malungs hembygdsgård

On the southern end of the town, the hembygdsgård, or Outdoor Museum, can be found. During the summer months, various events can be found here including the folk dancing the area is famous for.

## Restaurants & Bars

### Pizzeria Amore

Great little pizza place in the Swedish style, a little out of town. One of the rare places in Sweden offering deep dish as well as thin-crust pizza.
