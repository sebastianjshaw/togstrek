Trollhättan, located in southwestern Sweden, is a charming town known for its rich industrial history, scenic landscapes, and vibrant cultural scene. Nestled along the Göta River, Trollhättan offers a blend of natural beauty and urban amenities, making it an ideal destination for both relaxation and exploration. The town is renowned for its impressive locks and waterfalls, as well as its thriving film industry, earning it the nickname "Trollywood." Whether you're wandering through its picturesque parks, delving into local history, or enjoying a cozy café, Trollhättan provides a delightful Swedish experience.

## Sights & Culture

### Trollhättans kyrka

The main church in Trollhättan is easily visible from more or less anywhere.

### Skulpturpromenaden

Skulpturpromenaden, or the Sculpture Walk, is an outdoor gallery showcasing an array of contemporary sculptures by various artists. This cultural attraction offers a leisurely stroll through Trollhättan's scenic landscapes, allowing you to enjoy art in the open air. Each sculpture adds a unique element to the surroundings, making the walk both visually stimulating and thought-provoking.

### Järnvägsbron

Järnvägsbron, the historic railway bridge in Trollhättan, is an engineering marvel and a significant landmark. The bridge spans the Göta River and is an important part of the town's transportation history. Walking across the bridge provides stunning views of the river and the surrounding area, making it a favorite spot for photographers and history enthusiasts alike.

## Museums & Galleries

### Forngården

Forngården is a fascinating open-air museum that offers a glimpse into traditional Swedish rural life. The museum features historic buildings, including farmhouses and workshops, meticulously preserved to reflect their original state. Visitors can explore the exhibits and learn about the everyday lives of the people who once lived and worked in the region, making it an educational and immersive experience.

## Parks & Gardens

### Spikön

Spikön is a picturesque island park located in the middle of the Göta River. The park is a popular destination for both locals and tourists, offering walking trails, playgrounds, and picnic areas. With its lush greenery and tranquil setting, Spikön is perfect for a relaxing day out or a leisurely stroll by the river.

![](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/sweden/vastra-gotaland/trollhatten/20231216-Skulpturpromenaden-20231216-003A7109.jpg)

### Trollhättefallen

Trollhättefallen, the Trollhättan Falls, are a stunning series of waterfalls and locks that are central to the town's identity. The falls are part of the Göta River and play a crucial role in the local hydroelectric power generation. Visitors can witness the impressive water flow during scheduled releases and explore the surrounding area, which offers spectacular views and informative exhibits about the falls' history and engineering.

### **Betty Backs Park**

Betty Backs Park is a charming green space in Trollhättan, named after a beloved local figure. The park features beautifully landscaped gardens, walking paths, and seating areas where visitors can relax and enjoy the serene environment. It’s an ideal spot for a quiet afternoon or a casual meetup with friends.

## Restaurants & Bars

### Espresso House

Espresso House is a popular café chain in Sweden, known for its cozy atmosphere and high-quality coffee. The Trollhättan location offers a comfortable setting where you can enjoy a wide selection of beverages, pastries, and light meals. Whether you’re looking for a quick caffeine fix or a place to unwind with a book, Espresso House provides a welcoming retreat.

### Café Hundfikahembakat

Café Hundfikahembakat is a unique café in Trollhättan that welcomes both humans and their canine companions. The café serves homemade pastries and delicious coffee, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. It's the perfect spot for pet owners to relax and enjoy a treat while their dogs socialize.
