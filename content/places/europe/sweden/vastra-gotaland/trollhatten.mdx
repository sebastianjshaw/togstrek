Trollhättan, in southwestern Sweden, is known for its industrial history, its surprising landscapes and its film industry. It sits on the Göta River, close to its twin town Vänersborg, and has earned the nickname "Trollywood."

## Sights & Culture

### Trollhättans kyrka

The main church in Trollhättan is easily visible from more or less anywhere.

### Skulpturpromenaden

Skulpturpromenaden, or the Sculpture Walk, is an outdoor gallery of contemporary sculptures by various artists. If the weather is good, it is a fun walk. In mid-winter it was a less engaging activity.

### Järnvägsbron

Järnvägsbron, the historic railway bridge, is a significant landmark in Trollhättan. It spans the Göta River and is an important part of the town's transport history. Walking across it gives you great views of the river and the surrounding area.

## Museums & Galleries

### Forngården

Forngården is an open-air museum, something Sweden loves, that gives a glimpse of traditional Swedish rural life. Its historic buildings, including farmhouses and workshops, have been preserved as they were. You can explore the exhibits and learn about the everyday lives of the people who once lived and worked in the region.

![Forngården, Trollhättan](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/sweden/vastra-gotaland/trollhatten/20231216-Forng%C3%A5rden-20231216-003A7130.jpg)

## Parks & Gardens

### Spikön

Spikön is a small island park in the middle of the Göta River. It is popular with locals and visitors alike, and fairly busy even in bad weather, with walking trails, playgrounds and picnic areas.

![Spikön, Trollhättan](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/sweden/vastra-gotaland/trollhatten/20231216-Spik%C3%B6ns-20231216-003A7079.jpg)

### Trollhättefallen

Trollhättefallen, the Trollhättan Falls, are a series of waterfalls and locks at the centre of the town's identity, and the most surprising and impressive thing about Trollhättan. The falls are part of the Göta River, close to where it leaves Lake Vänern, and they play a crucial role in local hydroelectric power generation.

### Betty Backs Park

Betty Backs Park is a green space named after a local figure. The landscaped gardens, walking paths and seating areas are all in great condition.

![Betty Backs Park, Trollhättan](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/sweden/vastra-gotaland/trollhatten/20231216-Betty%20Backs%20Park-20231216-003A7156.jpg)

## Restaurants & Bars

### Café Hundfikahembakat

Café Hundfikahembakat is a café in Trollhättan that welcomes both humans and their dogs. It served homemade pastries and coffee, as well as snacks for the dogs, in a warm and inviting atmosphere, though a little small for a dog of Merlin's size. Since this review was written, the café has closed down.
