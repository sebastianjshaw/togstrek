Branäs is about five hours drive from [Gothenburg](/europe/sweden/vastra-gotaland/gothenburg) and is a small town with a reasonably sized ski resort.

## Sports & Activities

### Branäs Ski Resort

More focused on families than apres ski, there is still a fairly lively bar which runs late offering food and drinks, and gets into a good natured conga at the end of the evening. 

There is one primary hill with a long gondola up, and then a series of chair lifts half way down to take you back to the top. Both the front and back of the hill is utilised.  There is a decent selection of Red and Blue runs, it is an ideal location for a weekend away from Gothenburg, but don't go expecting the Sarenne run.
