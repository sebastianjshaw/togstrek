### Bran Castle

Whilst in Romania we explored the Bran Castle, reputed to be Vlad the Impalers castle. At the foot is a market selling a range of things, I ended up with a somewhat tacky sword.

The castle itself is good, not very large and likely a small outpost, that has been built on over the time, but worth the trip.
