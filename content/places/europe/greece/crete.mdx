A group holiday to Crete let us explore a great deal of the island. Based in a hotel near the beach on the western end of the island by **Chania**, we rented a car an drove the east-west road a few times seeing a number of locations.

There are a larger number of caves on the island to explore.

In the far east of the island we stopped to see **Lake Voulismeni**, the bottomless lake.

We stopped at **Knossos**, an impressive set of ruins and the seat of Minoan civilization. There was also the **Palace of Faistos**, in the south of the island, much harder to get to as the roads outside of the main east-west backbone are basic.
