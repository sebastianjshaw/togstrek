The final day, we woke up, ate breakfast and loaded onto the tour bus to Sophia. A brief stop again at a service station showed me a huge Communist style statue.

When we got to Sophia, our first stop was the Cathedral of St. Alexandra, and inside is his magical finger bone.

![20130202-002.jpg](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/bulgaria/sofia/20130202-002.jpg)

![20130202-003.jpg](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/bulgaria/sofia/20130202-003.jpg)

We carried on through the markets, the park, natural history museum and Roman museum.

We stopped for lunch at the Happy bar for some dark larger and walked down to the Mall, where I picked up a nice new camera bag, something perfect for a tablet and a 1D.
