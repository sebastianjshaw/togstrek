After everyone had boarded we set off on the crystal blue ocean, the first sight we saw, the **Statue of St. Paul** overlooking the island where he came to write much of his religious work.

![](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/malta/gozo/20131015+-+Gozo,+Comino+Boat+Trip+-+003-2.jpg)

_Statue of St. Paul_

We then arrived in Gozo and went on a bus tour around this small island with its tiny population. We saw **Fungus Rock, Crocodile Rock, the Azure Window** and the **Inland Sea.**
