Having seen much of [Gozo](/europe/malta/gozo), a few of us, ditched the big boat and took a small speedboat to Comino run by a bronzed man and his little dog. 

![Canon Canon EOS 5D Mark III EF24-105mm f/4L IS USM, 105mm, f11, 1/200, ISO400](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/malta/comino/20131015+-+Gozo,+Comino+Boat+Trip+-+001.jpg)

On route we passed through several caves including one with remarkable pink Coral.

![Canon Canon EOS 5D Mark III EF24-105mm f/4L IS USM, 105mm, f9, 1/40, ISO400](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/malta/comino/20131015+-+Gozo,+Comino+Boat+Trip+-+003.jpg)

Comino is a tiny island with a couple of beach-front cafés and a single hotel. The island is desolate but peaceful.

Comino is known for two things in particular. Firstly, the **Blue Lagoon**; warm, clear waters.

![Canon Canon EOS 5D Mark III EF17-40mm f/4L USM, 17mm, f16, 1/100, ISO160](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/malta/comino/20131015+-+Gozo,+Comino+Boat+Trip+-+006-2.jpg)

![Canon Canon EOS 5D Mark III EF17-40mm f/4L USM, 17mm, f16, 1/125, ISO160](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/malta/comino/20131015+-+Gozo,+Comino+Boat+Trip+-+005-2.jpg)

![Canon Canon EOS 5D Mark III EF24-105mm f/4L IS USM, 24mm, f8, 1/400, ISO160](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/malta/comino/20131015+-+Gozo,+Comino+Boat+Trip+-+004.jpg)

The second thing that Comino is known for is the **Fort** sat at the highest point of the island. The fort was prominently featured in the 2002 movie, the Count of Monte Cristo. I took the opportunity to get a bit closer to the Fort, but could not get too close as we were only stopped at the island for a short time.

![Canon Canon EOS 5D Mark III EF70-200mm f/4L USM +2x, 400mm, f8, 1/500, ISO160](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/malta/comino/20131015+-+Gozo,+Comino+Boat+Trip+-+004-2.jpg)

![Canon Canon EOS 5D Mark III EF24-105mm f/4L IS USM, 50mm, f8, 1/200, ISO160](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/malta/comino/20131015+-+Gozo,+Comino+Boat+Trip+-+005.jpg)

![Canon Canon EOS 5D Mark III EF17-40mm f/4L USM, 40mm, f8, 1/400, ISO160](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/malta/comino/20131015+-+Gozo,+Comino+Boat+Trip+-+006.jpg)

![Canon Canon EOS 5D Mark III EF70-200mm f/4L USM +2x, 400mm, f8, 1/1250, ISO160](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/malta/comino/20131015+-+Gozo,+Comino+Boat+Trip+-+007-2.jpg)

We then got back onto the large boat and headed back to the hotel, past the Elephant's Head rock.
