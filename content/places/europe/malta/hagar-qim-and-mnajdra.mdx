I then got off at the place that was my main purpose for this trip the neolithic temples of Hagar Qim and Mnajdra.

![](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/malta/hagar-qim-and-mnajdra/20131018+-+Hop+On+Hop+Off+Bus+-+010.jpg)

These ancient temples predate the pyramids of Egypt. Stonehenge or any other megalith.

Much of them remains a mystery. How on a small island that never kept slaves in Egypt's numbers were they able to build these structures. One block is 20 tonnes. The average weight of a stone in the pyramid is only 2.5 tonnes.
