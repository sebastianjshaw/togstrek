Luxembourg was an occasional trip when I lived in the Netherlands. It's long enough ago that I don't have a single photograph from any of it — well before digital cameras, well before my obsession with photography, and well before the cultural habit of documenting everything.

I remember little that's distinct about the country itself, which is exactly why it needs a return visit. I did Speech and Debate tournaments at rival schools in Luxembourg as part of ECIS, the kind of trip where you see a classroom, a coach, and very little else. More vividly, we collected our family dog here, from a Tibetan Terrier breeder in the countryside — for whatever reason, we'd all decided our dog must be a Tibetan Terrier, and that was that.

One of the unsung benefits of building this site has turned out to be the crystallising of a place: freezing it in time with photographs and stories so I can recapture it whenever I choose. Luxembourg is one of the gaps — a tiny war against the slow degradation of memory, still to be won.
