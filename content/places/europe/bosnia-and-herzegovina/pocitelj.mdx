Perched on a hillside along the [Neretva](/europe/bosnia-and-herzegovina/neretva) River, this hidden gem is a testament to the region's rich history and architectural beauty. As we climbed the the narrow, cobbled streets, I couldn't help but feel captivated by the blend of medieval and Ottoman influences that have shaped this charming settlement over the centuries.

## Sights & Culture

### Šišman Ibrahim-pašina džamija (Šišman Ibrahim Pasha Mosque)

The Šišman Ibrahim Pasha Mosque, a beautiful example of Ottoman architecture, is one of the most significant landmarks in Počitelj. Constructed in the 16th century, this historic mosque has withstood the test of time and continues to serve as a place of worship for the local community. As I stood in the courtyard, admiring the intricate stonework and elegant minaret, I felt a sense of reverence for the devotion and craftsmanship that went into creating this sacred space.

![](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/bosnia-and-herzegovina/pocitelj/20230605-Pocitelj-20230605-003A0201-HDR.jpg)

### Šišman Ibrahim-pašina medresa (Šišman Ibrahim Pasha Madrasa)

Just a short walk from the mosque, I discovered the Šišman Ibrahim Pasha Madrasa, an important Islamic education centre in Počitelj. Established in the same era as the mosque, this historic institution has been crucial in preserving the region's religious and cultural heritage. The building itself is a testament to the architectural prowess of the time, featuring a harmonious blend of traditional and Ottoman styles.

![](https://media.togstrek.com/europe/bosnia-and-herzegovina/pocitelj/20230605-Pocitelj-20230605-003A0183-HDR.jpg)

### Počiteljska utvrda (Počitelj Fortress)

Exploring further, I came across the impressive Počitelj Fortress, which stands guard over the village from its hilltop location. One of the highlights of my visit to Počitelj was climbing the stone steps leading up to the fortress that overlooks the village. From this vantage point, I was rewarded with breathtaking views of the surrounding countryside and the meandering river below. The experience left me with an appreciation for the resilience of its people, who have lovingly preserved their cultural heritage despite the challenges they've faced throughout history. This medieval fortification offers a glimpse into the region's past, as it has witnessed numerous battles and changes in power throughout the centuries.
