Jablanica, a quaint village in Bosnia, is home to a bridge that tells tales of a WWII battle. The bridge, now broken, stands as a testament to the past, a silent witness to the tumultuous history that unfolded here. As you tread the dirt path leading to the remains of the bridge, the air is filled with the sweet scent of flowers, a stark contrast to the sombre remnants of war. The preserved train nearby adds another layer to the historical narrative of the place. It's a poignant reminder of the passage of time, of stories that unfolded and were left behind. Jablanica, with its rich history and serene beauty, is a place that compels you to pause and reflect.

## Sights & Culture

### Neretva Bridge

In Jablanica, you'll find the remnants of the Neretva Bridge, a poignant reminder of the war that once consumed this region. This bridge, now broken and standing as a symbol of resilience, was once a vital connection across the Neretva River. It was here that a significant WWII battle unfolded, forever marking its place in history. As you approach, the scent of wildflowers wafting up from the dirt path adds a touch of tranquillity to the scene. The preserved train near the bridge's remains serves as a stark contrast to the serene landscape, a silent testament to the area's turbulent past. It's a place that prompts reflection, a spot where history and nature intertwine in a uniquely Bosnian narrative.

## Museums & Galleries

### Museum of the Battle for the Wounded at Neretva

The Museum of the Battle for the Wounded at Neretva in Jablanica is a poignant reminder of the harsh realities of war. This museum is dedicated to a significant event during World War II, where the Partisans had to make the difficult decision to destroy the Neretva Bridge in order to halt the advance of the Axis forces. The museum houses a collection of photographs, documents, and war relics that tell the story of this heroic act of resistance. The highlight of the museum is the preserved remains of the original bridge, a stark symbol of the sacrifices made during the war.
