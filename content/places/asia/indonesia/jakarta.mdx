When I lived in [Singapore](/asia/singapore/singapore), we travelled down to Jakarta en route to somewhere else. Where we were heading escapes me, but I have a vivid recollection of the scale and the heat of the city.

My memory paints it orange, under a bright blue sky, with white buildings all around.
