Former royal capital of Cambodia, on a hill about 40km northwest of Phnom Penh.

## Sights & Culture

### Phnom Oudong

A steep climb up the hill — they call it 500 steps — and you may well pass some monkeys on the way. Once up, there's a fair few stele to see. Worth seeing, but Sambor Prei Kuk is the better detour if you have to choose one.
