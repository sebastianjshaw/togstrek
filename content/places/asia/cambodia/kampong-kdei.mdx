## Sights & Culture

### Kampong Kdei Bridge

An impressive ancient bridge, worth a stop if you're passing on the Phnom Penh–Siem Reap road, but not worth a special detour on its own. A few touristy stalls line the approach, including one enterprising woman who'll photograph you without asking as you arrive, then show you a printout of your face with the bridge in the background. Don't feel pressured to pay.
