## Practical Information

### Tuk tuks

Use the **PassApp** or **Grab** apps and the price is fixed upfront, often just a 2–3 minute wait anywhere in town. Street hailing requires bargaining, but they're pretty happy to do so.

### Phnom Penh Airport

New and huge. It's far bigger than currently needed, and clearly built for future growth. Clean, modern, and fast to get through.

### Money

USD and Cambodian riel are used interchangeably. ATMs dispense both. Bring plenty of $1 bills for tips and small fares, as you won't see them much in change. Notes with any tears are rejected.
