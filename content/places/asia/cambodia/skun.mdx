A small market town an hour or so north-east of Phnom Penh.

## Sights & Culture

### Spider Market

A regular side-quest along the route to Angkor Wat is the Spider Market, where they sell deep-fried tarantulas, crickets, and grasshoppers. It sounds mysterious and strange. In fact it's a largely empty car park with a few vendors and sales people walking round with platters. Not really worth paying extra for, but if it's included, it's fine.
