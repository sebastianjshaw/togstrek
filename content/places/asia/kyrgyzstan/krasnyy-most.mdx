Krasnyy Most sits in the shadow of the Tian Shan, the Celestial Mountains, where the landscape starts to show its geological age. No volcanoes, but million-year-old fissures in the rock mark where the earth has shifted and settled over timescales that make the Silk Road feel recent. It's the kind of place you pass through rather than stay in, but the setting does most of the work.

## Restaurants & Bars

### Food Zone

A canteen-style stop on the road that operates on a point-and-hope basis if you don't have the language: the choices are microwaved and the menu requires some goodwill to navigate. The coffee, however, is decent, which is not something you can always count on along this stretch. Useful to know it's there.
