A village at the eastern tip of Issyk-Kul where the lake narrows and the landscape opens out towards the Kazakh border. The mountains marking that boundary are visible clearly from here on a good day, a clean line of peaks that tells you exactly where one country ends and another begins. Worth a stop and a photograph, not much more than that.
