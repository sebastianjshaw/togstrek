A picnic stop that was part of a petrol station on the road to [Charyn Canyon](/asia/kazakhstan/charyn). Small wooden huts, a shop, and a cat with a can of sardines and absolutely no interest in the group. The drop toilet nearby is blue-walled concrete, structural, and detectable from ten metres in any wind direction. Use it only if necessary and even then you might regret it.
