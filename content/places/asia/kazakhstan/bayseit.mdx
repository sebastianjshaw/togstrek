A roadside stop on the drive between [Saty](/asia/kazakhstan/saty) and [Almaty](/asia/kazakhstan/almaty), the kind of town that exists to serve the road rather than the other way around. Worth knowing what's there.

---

## Sights & Culture

### Park memorial

A memorial in the small park near the town centre.

---

## Shopping

### Жетiсу Сауда Supermarket

A decent sized supermarket, good for stocking up for the road. We stocked up for lunch on the move. Pasties, crisps, Oreos. Not every meal on the Silk Road is a revelation.

### Market

A small market running mostly to fruit and vegetables. Fresh produce if you need it, nothing more ambitious than that.
