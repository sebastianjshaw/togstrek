When I think back to my childhood trip to Agadir, the memories are filled with the warmth and charm of this coastal Moroccan city. Our beach holiday was nothing short of magical in the way only early childhood memories can be, with endless days spent on the golden sands, where the sun was always hot and the sky, a brilliant blue. I vividly remember the feeling of the warm sand between my toes as I played along the shoreline, the strange sight of camels passing by and the cool water of the pool.
