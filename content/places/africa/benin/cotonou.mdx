Cotonou, the beating heart of Benin, is a place where the hum of French conversation fills the air, a testament to its colonial past, and a stark difference to the English-speaking countries of West Africa. The city is a sensory delight, with the aroma of delicious street food wafting through the air, a testament to the country's rich culinary heritage. The transition from Lagos to Cotonou is marked by the shift from 'bush meat' to baguettes with 'jambon', a symbol of the city's French influence. The city's vibrant nightlife and excellent shopping scene make it a must-visit destination. But it's not just the city's hustle and bustle that leaves an impression. The memories of singing karaoke in a Chinese restaurant at midnight, and the tranquillity of a hotel swimming pool, serve as reminders of the city's ability to cater to a wide range of experiences. Cotonou, with its blend of cultures and experiences, is a city that leaves a lasting impression.
