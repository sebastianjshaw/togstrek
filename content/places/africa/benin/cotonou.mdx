Cotonou is the capital and heart of Benin, and starkly different to the English-speaking countries of West Africa it neighbours. The transition from Lagos to Cotonou is marked by the shift from ‘bush meat’ to baguettes with ‘jambon’, a symbol of the city’s French influence.

It’s a city I’d love to return to. I was there on a work trip, and so exploration was limited. My memories are of singing karaoke in a Chinese restaurant at midnight, and the tranquillity of a hotel swimming pool the day after.
