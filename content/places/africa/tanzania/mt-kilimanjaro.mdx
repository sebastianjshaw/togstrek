Kilimanjaro is the highest free-standing mountain in the world, and Uhuru Peak, at 5,895 metres, is the highest point in Africa. In March 2022 I climbed it by the Machame route, known as the “Whiskey route”, over six days: up through rainforest, moorland and alpine desert, to the summit before sunrise, and down to Mweka Gate.

I travelled with [G Adventures](https://gadventures.com/), and their guides and porters were the reason I reached the top. I turned 42 on the slopes, had high-altitude birthday cake, and made a layering mistake on the final ascent that involved taking most of my clothes off at 2 am. That one is covered on Day 5.

The six days add up to roughly 62 km, from 1,640 metres at Machame Gate to the summit and back down to the forest at Mweka. Every day has its own page with the distance, altitude, camps and what the trail was like.
