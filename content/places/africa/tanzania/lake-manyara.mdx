## Experience

Our first stage of the safari was Lake Manyara National Park, just south of Mto Wa Mbu. We loaded into our two jeeps and headed out with the top raised and the windows open ready to start seeing the wildlife. The park is beautiful with thick trees, tall hills and deep water.

![Canon Canon EOS R5 EF100-400mm f/4.5-5.6L IS II USM, 100mm, f8, 1/3200, ISO2500](https://media.togstrek.com/africa/tanzania/lake-manyara/Misc-20220313-0001.jpg)

![Canon Canon EOS R5 EF24-105mm f/4L IS USM, 24mm, f9, 1/1600, ISO10000](https://media.togstrek.com/africa/tanzania/lake-manyara/Misc-20220313-0002.jpg)

![Canon Canon EOS R5 EF100-400mm f/4.5-5.6L IS II USM, 100mm, f8, 1/1600, ISO1250](https://media.togstrek.com/africa/tanzania/lake-manyara/Misc-20220313-0003.jpg)

![Canon Canon EOS R5 EF100-400mm f/4.5-5.6L IS II USM, 400mm, f8, 1/1600, ISO500](https://media.togstrek.com/africa/tanzania/lake-manyara/Misc-20220313-0004.jpg)

![Canon Canon EOS R5 EF100-400mm f/4.5-5.6L IS II USM, 100mm, f8, 1/1600, ISO1000](https://media.togstrek.com/africa/tanzania/lake-manyara/Misc-20220313-0005.jpg)

![Canon Canon EOS R5 EF100-400mm f/4.5-5.6L IS II USM, 100mm, f8, 1/1600, ISO640](https://media.togstrek.com/africa/tanzania/lake-manyara/Misc-20220313-0006.jpg)

Almost immediately we came across an elephant in the middle of the road that came incredibly close to our car. Moving closer to the lake, we found a herd of Impala, a wild herd of Zebra and a pair of Red Bookbills fighting with an Eagle over some carrion. We also saw the famous sausage tree.

![](https://media.togstrek.com/africa/tanzania/lake-manyara/Sausage+Tree-20220313-0001.jpg)

As we drove around, baboons swarmed the road, as well as the Blue Balls Monkey.

At a lake, you’ll see a lot of bird life, most magnificent perhaps were the Grey Crowned Cranes.

When we finished for the day, it was straight to a campsite for some dinner and a general hang out.

## Location

A large national park south of Mto Wa Mbu, the park’s central feature is the huge lake, drawing in wild animals and birds from miles around.
