We then wandered around town for a while just getting a feel for the place. Santa Monica is awesome, it has a really good vibe!

## Sights & Culture

### Santa Monica PIer

One of the most photographed locations in the world, Santa Monica Pier is a tourist heaven. Full of shops, activities and restaurants, it stretches out into the ocean. At night it is brightly lit with colourful LEDs.

## Restaurants & Bars

### Bubba Gump Shrimp

A chain restaurant based on a chain from a movie. Peculiar idea. The waiter guy was very impressed I had three pints with my meal, which appears to be a lot in the states.

![](https://media.togstrek.com/north-america/usa/california-santa-monica/20100921-_js_1494jpg_5174666688_o.jpg)

I have found the American beer is actually fine. There are lots of bad beers around, but that's true in the UK too, Fosters is everywhere! Sam Adams is a good beer, had a few other local ones. It's the wine that seems to be the issue. Very expensive, most is about 9 dollars a glass, so at least 6 quid, and it's not great...

### Cheesecake Factory

Everything you’ve ever heard about American portions is embodied in the Cheesecake factory. A vast menu of choices and the eponymous cheesecake is incredible, but literally thousands of calories a slice.
