The **Washington Monument** is the obelisk anchor of the Mall, marble, lightning rods, and endless tourists finding the sun in their eyes. The material that defines this entry is photographic: **black and white** negatives and prints, including at least one **double exposure** where the shaft overlaps itself or the sky twice. Digitise the frames, note the film stock if it’s recorded, and this stub can grow into a proper darkroom-era companion to the colour trips that came later.
