**Busch Gardens, Williamsburg** in the early 1990s still traded heavily on the **Old Country** theming, half timber, “countries” as lands, and the kind of parking infrastructure that produced a physical ticket stub. This stub is the anchor for the page: a **1991** receipt from the lot, proof of a hot August day spent on rides and walkways rather than in colonial costume. Expand later with scans and whatever rides still exist under new names.
