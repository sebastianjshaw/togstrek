The **American Museum of Natural History** faces **Central Park West**; in the early 1990s the **Hayden Planetarium** was still the older sphere-and-theatre complex that a generation associated with whales, dinosaurs, and a dark room full of stars. Stub for a **1991** visit, pair it with the Intrepid entry for the same childhood circuit through Manhattan.
