In 2016 I had to go to New York for work. It was something of a flying visit, landing on Monday and heading off Thursday afternoon, so there was not much time for exploring photography. I still managed to get a few shots after work primarily on the Lower East Side where we were staying. I walked around the Sara D. Roosevelt Park, a peculiar park that is a block-wide but about five or more blocks long covering gardens, basketball and football (soccer) spaces and a few other bits and pieces.

## Restaurants & Bars

### Katz’s Delicatessen

Famous around the world, Katz’s deli is an institution. Locals and tourists blend in the busy, friendly space. Even Spider-Man is known to drop by for pastrami.

The food is incredible.

## 1991 museums

Years before the 2016 work week on the Lower East Side, the same city showed up on a childhood itinerary. Two stops from that trip now have their own stubs:

- [Intrepid Sea, Air & Space Museum](/north-america/united-states-of-america/new-york/intrepid-sea-air-space-museum) — **46th St & 12th Ave**, carrier decks and cold war hardware on the Hudson.
- [American Museum of Natural History](/north-america/united-states-of-america/new-york/american-museum-natural-history) — **Central Park West**, including the **Hayden Planetarium** as it was in the early 1990s.
