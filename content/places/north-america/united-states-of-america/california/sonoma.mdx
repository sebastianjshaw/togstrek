_On the American highway to Sonoma_

We arrived in Sonoma and checked into the hotel, which was really nice. Old for America, having been built in the eighteen hundreds. It had a rooftop pool that we used after dark and ancient style lift, with brass grates and everything.  
  
We wandered out to find some food at about 8pm and found that everything had closed much earlier. It was deserted. We finally found a small pizza place with hot waitress who stayed open late to feed us, which was much appreciated. She also gave us directions to a rock bar that was open where we could have a few drinks and listen to some music.
