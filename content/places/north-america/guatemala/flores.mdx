A quaint little island town nestled in the heart of Lake Petén Itzá in Guatemala. It's a place that's as colourful as a painter's palette, with its colonial-style houses dressed in a rainbow of hues. The narrow, winding streets are a photographer's dream, each turn revealing a new snapshot of local life.

But what makes Flores truly special is its location. It's like a gateway to the past, with some of the most significant Maya sites just a stone's throw away. Imagine waking up to the sunrise over [Tikal](/north-america/guatemala/tikal), or spending the afternoon exploring the ancient art and architecture of Yaxhá. It's like stepping into another world, a world that whispers tales of a time long gone.

But here's the thing - Flores is a town on borrowed time. It's slowly sinking, succumbing to the very lake that cradles it. The streets, once bustling with life, now bear the scars of frequent floods. It's a poignant reminder of the delicate balance between man and nature. But for now, Flores stands, a vibrant testament to resilience and charm in the face of adversity.

## Sights & Culture

### Castillo Arizmendi

The Castillo Arizmendi, located in Flores, is a historical site that dates back to the Spanish colonial era. It was constructed as a fortress-prison and was completed in the year 1700. The architecture of the castle reflects the colonial influence and the strategic importance of the location during that period. The castle has stood the test of time and is a testament to the rich history and culture of the region. Today, it serves as a popular tourist attraction, offering visitors a glimpse into the past and an opportunity to learn about the region's colonial history.

### Entrada a Malecon Isla De Flores

The Entrada a Malecon Isla de Flores is a popular spot in Flores. It is the entrance to the Malecon, a vibrant and bustling boardwalk area on the island of Flores. The Malecon is known for its beautiful views of the lake, colourful buildings, and lively atmosphere. It is lined with numerous shops, restaurants, and cafes, making it a favourite spot for both locals and tourists. The entrance itself is a welcoming sight, leading visitors into the heart of the island's social and cultural life.

### **Catedral Nuestra Señora de Los Remedios**

The Catedral Nuestra Señora de Los Remedios is a significant religious site in Flores, Guatemala. It is a beautiful cathedral that stands as a symbol of the city's deep-rooted Catholic faith. The cathedral is known for its stunning architecture, intricate designs, and peaceful ambience. It is a place of worship, reflection, and spiritual solace for many. The Catedral Nuestra Señora de Los Remedios is not just a religious site but also a cultural landmark that adds to the charm and character of the city.

![](https://media.togstrek.com/north-america/guatemala/flores/20221216-20221216+-+Flores--0001.jpg)

### **The Bridge**

The Bridge in Flores, Guatemala, is a significant structure that connects the island of Flores to the mainland. It serves as a vital link for transportation and communication between the island and the rest of the country. The bridge is a symbol of connectivity and unity, bridging the gap between different parts of the region. It is also a popular spot for tourists, offering stunning views of the surrounding lake and cityscape, but the advice is to stay on the Island side as night falls.

## Restaurants & Bars

### Mangos Bar

Perched atop the **Hotel Isla de Flores**, Mango's Bar is a spot that's all about the views. As the sun dips below the horizon, the panorama across the island is nothing short of breathtaking. It's the kind of place where you can't help but pause and take a moment to appreciate the beauty of it all. The menu is a delightful mix of international favourites with a sprinkling of local flair. But what really stands out are their cocktails. They're bold, they're adventurous, and they're just the thing to sip on as you soak in the sunset.

And for beer lovers, Mango's Bar doesn't disappoint. Their beer on tap comes in two sizes - huge and vast. It's a clear message that here, they believe in going big or going home.

### Skybar

Skybar in Flores is a bit of a surprise package. You order a pizza expecting a modest personal size, and what lands on your table is a medium that's absolutely massive. It makes you wonder, just how big is their large pizza? It's a mystery that might just need solving on a return visit.

The place itself has a great vibe, with a view that's nothing short of impressive. It's easy to see why it's a favourite spot for the locals to bring their dates. There's something about the twinkling lights and the laid-back atmosphere that just sets the mood right.

And let's not forget their nachos. When they say 'plate of nachos', they mean a plate that's practically overflowing. It's a mountain of crunchy goodness that's sure to satisfy even the biggest of appetites. Skybar is definitely a place to check out when you're in Flores. Just make sure you bring your appetite with you.

### **Delirio**

Delirio, a quaint little spot in town, is the kind of place that makes you feel all warm and fuzzy inside. It's got this charming, almost twee vibe that's just too cute to resist and you half expect to see Harry and Cho on an awkward date there.

Now, let's talk about their coffee. It's the kind of coffee that makes you sit up and take notice. It's the kind of coffee that makes you want to savour every last drop.

And the food? Oh, the food. Their croissants are a dream. Flaky, buttery, and just the right amount of crisp. It's the kind of pastry that makes you close your eyes in delight. But the star of the show has to be their French toast. It's sweet, it's indulgent, and it's the perfect way to start your day.

So, if you're in the mood for a little bit of whimsy with your breakfast, Delirio is the place to be.

## Shopping

The south-west corner of the island has a number of souvenirs shops, with wood carvings and stonework for sale. Naturally carvings of Tikal are the most common.

## Sports & Activities

### Swimming

All around the island, there are easy spots to jump into the lake and have a swim. The water is warm though its important to keep an eye out for the various boats running people around.

### Sunset Cruise

Our evening kicked off with a Sunset Cruise. We hopped aboard a flat raft-style boat, decked out with LED lights that added a bit of sparkle to the setting sun. The boat was kitted out with seats and a makeshift dance floor, and a booming speaker that kept the tunes flowing.

As for the drinks, they were flowing freely, perhaps a bit too freely for some. A word of advice for future cruisers - the lack of bathroom facilities on board might make you rethink that extra beer.
