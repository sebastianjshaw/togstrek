Last week I got to put some of my studio practice into action. I went along to the [More Light](http://www.halfremembereddreams.co.uk/) rehearsal before they headed up to the [Edinburgh](/europe/united-kingdom/scotland/edinburg) Fringe to take a few headshots of the cast.

I used three lights, one softbox to the left of the actor, and above pointing downwards to bring in some Rembrandt lighting. One more softbox to the right, about 50% further away to fill in the light, and one barn door at the rear is used for a hair light.

It made it clear to me that my lights are not as adjustable as some of the more professional ones I've used in other places and I need more practice to get the lighting just right, it’s a little overpowered, and the Rembrandt shadow is largely missing. All their eyes have a clear catch-light however, so they are bringing the right level of attention.

All in all, good, but I could do better if I tried again.
