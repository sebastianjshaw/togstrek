I attended the first few days of the Fringe, its my second year, and I spent my time moving between comedy venues and theatres, with a bit of shooting in between. I went to the national monument and shot some photos during the evenings.

Unfortunately, I couldn't use my camera in any of the Venues.

Special recommendations go to Lucy Frederick, Lee Camp and Kelfi & Fikel. All funny in very different ways and worth watching. Titus Andronicus at C Venues Nova is worth watching as well if you aren't too squeamish!
