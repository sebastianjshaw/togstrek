# ‘If you can smell the street by looking at the photo, it’s a street photograph.’

_\- Bruce Gilden_

Mostly walks without a destination: Soho and a grey February afternoon in London, and after dark in Eriksberg in Gothenburg. I’m still working out what makes a street photograph rather than a photograph of a street, which is probably why I keep going back.
