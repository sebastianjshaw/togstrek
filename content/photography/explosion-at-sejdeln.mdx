The explosion drew a crowd, with many people pulling out their phones to capture the scene. Police had to intervene, pushing the onlookers back to a safe distance. Rumours quickly spread about a masked individual who allegedly threw a bomb into the pub's bathroom before fleeing the scene on an electric scooter.

According to the news reports, a man in his twenties has been arrested in connection with the incident. He denies any involvement in the crime. The investigation is ongoing, and any motive behind the attack remains unclear.

The explosion has left the community in shock, and the pub, a popular local spot, now stands silent and damaged. It's a stark reminder of the unpredictability of life, even in a place as familiar as your local pub.
