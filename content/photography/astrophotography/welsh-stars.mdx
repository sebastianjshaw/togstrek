On holiday, camping in Wales, I decided, with limited equipment (a table was my tripod) to try and capture some of the stars, as there was no light pollution. I had to remember a fair bit of things I'd learned in the past, and wish I had remembered more. A really good, comprehensive answer on taking good photographs is provided [Stack Exchange](https://photo.stackexchange.com/questions/43028/how-to-have-colors-in-milky-way).
