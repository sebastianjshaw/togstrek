After a wander, we headed to the nearby Bakery. Which is a full on industrial bakery but they put a selection of there wares out for locals on an honour system. I suspect many of the most delicious items vanish early.

![](https://media.togstrek.com/photography/avalon-visit-2/20240907_154901.jpg)

I then drove around the Sikhall Campground and bathing spot and had a lovely cup of coffee from their restaurant/cafe/store. They’ve also got toilets and showers here, which is useful to know for a potential landowner who has no facilities on the land at all.

![](https://media.togstrek.com/photography/avalon-visit-2/20240907_163926.jpg)
