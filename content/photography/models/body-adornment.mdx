This was a meetup of the Alternative Photographer and Models Meetup Group.

![68.jpg](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6207d70ece223e42dd9ae587/1645464040242-TBMBYWFZI3CLAUIOIAIS/68.jpg)

![53.jpg](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6207d70ece223e42dd9ae587/1645464038377-QOLMKTG0W24V9VGOB27C/53.jpg)

Three models attended:

1.  [Amy Tall](http://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100001691734829)
    
2.  [Amy Harding](http://www.modelmayhem.com/1811310)
    
3.  [Kendra Lee](http://www.modelmayhem.com/1530274)
    

Amy Tall is, as her name implies, extremely tall, very thin and very tattooed. She is very unusual to shoot as a model, therefore, Amy Harding is much shorter, Kendra has a somewhat California girl look.

There were two setups, a high key main setup using the White Infinity curve and a low key using a fairly simple barn door and a grey background,

We shot all three models on both setups.

Unfortunately, the fourth booked model, a Drag King, was a no-show, which meant the days ran a little shorter than it should have
