### ANDRA LÅNGGATANS SKIVHANDEL

Surrounded by records, and tightly lit the two bands played to a good-sized crowd.

## Boy with Apple

Listen to them on Spotify [Boy with Apple](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6cs5N6HLaTJIgJefiYLkg7?si=lsTw6yY5TtWFxJAk9JtRMA&nd=1)

> Dreampop/Shoegaze from Gothenburg. Boy With Apple consists of Saga Fransson, Zara Henriksson and Tim Hedlund.

A softer sound than Klass II, their sound worked well in tight spaces.
