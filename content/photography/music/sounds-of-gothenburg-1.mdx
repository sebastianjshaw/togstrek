_Sounds of Gothenburg_

I was invited along to the premiere of Sounds of [Gothenburg](/europe/sweden/vastra-gotland/gothenburg) to take some photos by a friend of mine. Looking back through the archive, it had been a decade since I last shot a gig.

When I arrived, I was impressed by the team's kit for the event, two professional video cameras, and a DSLR, combined with a solid audio setup. I felt a little unnecessary.

I made an effort to take more photos of the backstage as the setup was already going to provide strong images of the artists. The venue was challenging as the sunlight outside was dimmed by grey clouds, and despite the large windows, I was pushing ISO more than is ideal. I'm not entirely happy with the results.

First up was [Jaded Jane](https://www.instagram.com/jadedjane/?hl=en), singing alongside [Nadja Häikiö Itäsaari](https://www.nadjaitasaari.com/), one of the organisers. His music was haunting, self-described atmospheric pop, with a surprising use of a kazoo.

## Day Felice

![20210710-_JS_9458-9458.jpg](https://media.togstrek.com/photography/music/sounds-of-gothenburg-1/20210710-_JS_9458-9458.jpg)

![20210710-5DM33640-33640.jpg](https://media.togstrek.com/photography/music/sounds-of-gothenburg-1/20210710-5DM33640-33640.jpg) 

[Day Felice](https://www.instagram.com/ofdayfelice/?hl=en) follows a contrast pop quartet fronted by actor David Lindström who you may recognise from Vikings. They joke that all their songs are about depression, but they are a blend of darkness and light in true contrast.
