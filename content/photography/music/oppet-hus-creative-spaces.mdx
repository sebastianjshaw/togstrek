## T & The Rex

![1440×1920 (Exif image size)](https://media.togstrek.com/photography/music/oppet-hus-creative-spaces/T&The+Rex-20230617-0001.jpg)

With clever and deeply thought lines, T & The Rex establish their credentials as a skilled and provoking songwriter. The music is easy to listen to and powerful in its message.

Follow her on Spotify here: [T & The Rex](https://open.spotify.com/artist/24IVfAcshZ9VQeNnneWlUz?si=sxXxp2yMSBWfRnopViLGAQ&dl_branch=1&nd=1)

## **Lovisa Lago**

![1920×1440 (Exif image size)](https://media.togstrek.com/photography/music/oppet-hus-creative-spaces/Creative+Spaces+Open+House-20230617-0001.jpg)

Performing two of her own songs and the now famous Tattoo from Sweden’s Eurovision win, Lovisa’s powerful voice dominated the room. Accompanied on guitar by her producer, the music swept through us.

Follow her on Spotify: [Lovisa Lago](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3efVzV31UnKxqolL9Hjar4?si=oc1SEYdxQemg1znl4wH75Q&utm_medium=share&utm_source=linktree&nd=1)

## Linnea from Day Felis

![1920×1440 (Exif image size)](https://media.togstrek.com/photography/music/oppet-hus-creative-spaces/Linnea+of+Day+Felis-20230617-0001.jpg)

Shockingly 18 months had passed between seeing [Day Felis live in Dirty Records](/photography/music/sounds-of-gothenburg-1) and seeing the lead singer Linnea perform. When speaking to her after the set I confidently asserted I’d seem Day Felis live only six months before. Its little wonder they looked at me a little lost.

Follow Day Felis here: [@ofdayfelice](https://www.instagram.com/ofdayfelice/?hl=en)
