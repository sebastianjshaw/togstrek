# The nude portrait is only incidentally about the naked person in the middle of the room.

_\- Paula Brook_

Two shoots, a couple of years apart. In the first I limited myself to a single light, a strip softbox and later a beauty dish, with an experienced model. In the second, in a Battersea studio, I shared an hour with another photographer: three ten-minute sessions each, with a model who was happy to try whatever I suggested.
