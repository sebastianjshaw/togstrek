# Beyond the seas, beyond the fields, beyond the mists, there lies Avalon, the land where dreams may rest and kings shall find their peace.

John Steinbeck

**Avalon** will be a unique off-grid retreat nestled in the natural beauty of Sweden, inspired by the mysticism of Arthurian legend. My goal is to create a sustainable haven that balances simplicity with nature, using permaculture principles to restore the land and foster biodiversity. The project embraces self-sufficiency through renewable energy, rainwater harvesting, and natural building techniques, with structures like **The Great Hall** ( a central asian yurt) and **The Smithy** (a traditional Swedish grillkåta) seamlessly blending into the landscape. With Avalon, I aim to build a living, breathing space that honours the land's natural rhythms while creating a magical, mystical atmosphere for future generations of dogs mostly. Follow along as I document the journey, from the first shovel in the ground to the vision of a thriving, eco-conscious sanctuary.
