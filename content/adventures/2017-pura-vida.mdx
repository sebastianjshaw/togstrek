# 2017: Pura Vida


### In 2017, I headed to Central America, to jump off of cliffs, explore waterfalls, surf, climb and cycle around Costa Rica.


I found a land living by the motto Pura Vida. 'pure Life' A way of living life to the fullest.


The full book is available on my blog, read through the whole experience or, if you prefer, order a hefty coffee table book with all the writing and images you see below to explore in your own time.
