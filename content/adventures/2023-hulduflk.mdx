## Summer in Iceland

2023 was a return to Iceland to see it in all its glory. I’d been on a long weekend visit once, hoping to see the northern lights, but much like the Hidden Folk, the lights were obscured by the clouds. This time, it was high summer and a lack of darkness meant there was no chance of seeing anything in the sky, so instead we looked to the landscape.

Travelling around the entire island on its singular ring road, the Huldufólk blessed us with incredible weather for our entire visit.
