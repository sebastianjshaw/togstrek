# 2013: Silent Cities and Blue Lagoons


### In late summer 2013, I took my 5D Mark III to Malta, Gozo and Comino to see the landscapes and ancient monuments.


This travel diary documents the week I spent walking around this small island and trying to capture its huge history.


[Buy the Book](https://www.blurb.com/b/5983065-a-tog-s-trek-silent-cities-and-blue-lagoons)


The full book is available on my blog, read through the whole experience or, if you prefer, order a hefty coffee table book with all the writing and images you see below to explore in your own time.
