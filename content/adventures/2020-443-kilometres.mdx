# 443 Kilometres

### With Corona making travel impossible and irresponsible, I took a month off of work and hiked the entire 443km of the King's Trail.

Starting in the far north of Sweden, the Arctic town of [Abisko](/europe/sweden/norbotten/abisko-ostra), and ending nearer the middle in [Hemavan](/europe/sweden/vasterbotten/hemavan), the trail moves through diverse terrain and is an incredible chance to really see what Sweden has to offer.

These posts outline, section by section, my experiences and observations hiking this trail.
